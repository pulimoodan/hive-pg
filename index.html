FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the rent and security deposit?

Rent starts from ₹3000/month: 6 sharing ₹3000, 3/4 sharing ₹3200, 2 sharing ₹4000. WiFi, electricity and water are all included — no extra charges. Security deposit is just ₹1000. Only mess food is optional and paid extra.

What is included in the rent?

Your monthly rent covers WiFi, electricity, water, laundry access, CCTV security and all common-area amenities. Mess food is the only optional extra — a paid outsourced add-on not included in rent.

Is food / mess available?

Yes — mess food is available as an optional extra paid outsourced service. It is not included in the monthly rent; you can opt in if you want home-style meals without cooking.

Are there time restrictions or a curfew?

No. We offer an independent atmosphere with no time restrictions — ideal for working professionals and students.

How far is it from Infopark and Edappally?

Infopark Phase 1 is around 6 km, Edappally is 3.4 km, Kalamassery is 5.5 km and Palarivattom is 4.5 km. We're in Kakkanad — the Thrikkakara Temple bus stop is just a 10-minute walk away.

Is parking available?

Yes, we have spacious and secure bike parking for residents.
